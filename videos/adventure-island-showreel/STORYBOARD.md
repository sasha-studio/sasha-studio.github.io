# Adventure Island Showreel

## Intent
A cinematic visual portfolio tour: establish the world, then reveal its maps, environments, game interface, art elements, and letter-character cast. English titles, no narration, approximately 70.68 seconds.

## Design
Adapted from the project DESIGN.md: night indigo canvas, warm paper headlines, starlight-gold chapter labels, Fraunces display and DM Sans utility typography. Art remains the focal point; transitions crossfade through adjacent chapters.
